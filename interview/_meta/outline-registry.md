# Outline Registry

Track the health and growth of lecture-style outlines.

## Format

- `[[interview/outlines/<topic>]]`
  - Topic: `<topic>`
  - Question count: `<n>`
  - Chapter count: `<n>`
  - Last reframed: `<date or none>`
  - Status: `growing | stable | needs-reframe`
